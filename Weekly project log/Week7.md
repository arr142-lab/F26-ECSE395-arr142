### Mechanical Prototype Development

The team now has a basic paper/cardboard prototype of the electronics enclosure. This prototype is being used to start thinking about the physical size, component placement, access, and overall form of the enclosure before making a more detailed version.

I also began developing the enclosure in SolidWorks. I started creating the box and backplate and worked on making the CAD model parametric. I set up global variables and equations so dimensions such as the box width, height, depth, wall thickness, and backplate dimensions can be changed without rebuilding the model.

Electrical prototyping also continued this week. We currently have electrical components and mechanical prototype components being developed, but they have not yet been integrated into a single prototype.
